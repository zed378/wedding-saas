# 03 - Personas

## Persona 1: Sarah, 27, Bride-to-Be (Primary)
- Office worker, organizing the wedding herself with her partner, medium budget.
- Digitally literate (active on Instagram, has used Canva before), but not technical.
- Motivation: the invitation should look professional, easy to share to a large family WhatsApp group.
- Common frustrations: templates that are too rigid, difficulty changing photos, unclear whether the payment succeeded.
- UX needs: a fast process, clear feedback at every step, a real-time preview that builds confidence before paying.

## Persona 2: Budi, 32, Wedding Organizer (Secondary)
- Manages 5-10 weddings per month, needs to create many invitations for different clients.
- UX needs: a clear multi-invitation dashboard, quick duplication/templating, easy RSVP report exports to send to clients.
- Common frustration: having to log in/out repeatedly if the platform doesn't handle multiple invitations per account well.

## Persona 3: Mrs. Ratna, 55, Parent of the Couple (Tertiary/Indirect)
- Not an app user, but a RECIPIENT of the invitation link.
- Not very tech-savvy, might open it from a phone with a slow connection.
- UX needs: the public page MUST load fast, clear date/location/maps info that's easy to read, an unambiguous RSVP button.

## Persona 4: Internal Admin (Rina, Operations Staff)
- Handles daily moderation & support, not very technical (not a developer).
- UX needs: the admin panel must be straightforward, fast search (by email/slug), moderation actions in 1-2 clicks.

## Design Implications
- The Editor is optimized for Sarah & Budi (power users, need efficiency).
- The public page is optimized for Mrs. Ratna & other general guests (simplicity, speed, accessibility).
